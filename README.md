# The History of Cigarette Advertising: From Cowboy Icons to Cultural Legends

Few advertising campaigns in history have been as influential or as long-lasting as those that defined the modern cigarette brand.

## The Early Days

In the early twentieth century, cigarettes were marketed as mild, refined products. Brands competed on taste and elegance rather than on image.

## The Marlboro Man Revolution

Everything changed in the 1950s when the Marlboro Man was introduced. The rugged cowboy, set against wide open spaces, redefined what a cigarette could represent: freedom, strength, and authenticity. This campaign transformed the brand and created a cultural icon that still resonates today.
